# Master pages, headers, and page numbers
<!-- keywords: master page, background page, header, footer, page number, two-page master, facing pages, running head, repeat on every page, logo on every page, apply master -->

A master page is a background page: whatever you put on it shows on every page that uses it. Use a master page for things that repeat, such as a logo, a header, a footer, or page numbers. Change the master once, and every page that uses it changes.

## Edit a master page

1. Click **View > Views > Master Page**.
2. The **Master Page** tab appears. Add or change text boxes, pictures, and shapes just as you would on a page. The status bar says which master you are on, such as "Master Page A".
3. Click **Master Page > Close > Close Master Page** when you are done.

Master objects show on your pages, but you cannot select them there. To change them, go back to the master.

## Add and manage master pages

On the **Master Page** tab, in the **Master Page** group, use **Add Master Page**, **Duplicate**, **Rename**, and **Delete**. Each master gets a letter: A, B, C, and so on. Deleting a master moves its pages to the first master. A publication always keeps at least one.

## Use a master on your pages

- **Page Design > Pages > Master Pages** lists your masters, such as "(A) Master Page A". Choose one for the current page, or choose **None**.
- On the **Master Page** tab, **Apply To** has **Apply to All Pages** and **Apply to Current Page**.
- Right-click a page in the page list, and choose **Master Pages**.

To edit a different master, apply it to a page first. Then click **View > Views > Master Page**.

## Two-page masters

Books have facing pages: a left page and a right page. Click **Master Page > Layout > Two-Page Master** to design both sides at once. Page 1 is a right-hand page, so odd pages use the right half and even pages use the left half. In the **Layout Guides** window, the **Margin Guides** tab then calls the side margins **Inside** and **Outside**.

## Add a header or footer

A header is text at the top of every page. A footer is text at the bottom.

1. Click **Insert > Header & Footer > Header** (or **Footer**).
2. JeffPub opens the master page and puts the cursor in a box above the top margin guide (or below the bottom one).
3. Type your text, then click **Close Master Page**.

## Add page numbers

1. Click **Insert > Header & Footer > Page Number**.
2. In the **Page Number** window, choose a **Position**, such as Bottom center, and a **Number format**: 1, 2, 3; i, ii, iii; I, II, III; or a, b, c.
3. Clear **Show page number on first page** if you do not want a number on a cover. JeffPub then makes a second master for page 1.
4. Click **OK**.

The number box goes on the master your current page uses, so it shows on every page that uses it. If your cursor is in a text box, **Page Number** (or Alt+Shift+P) puts the number there instead. The arrow beside the button offers **Insert Page Count**, for "Page 2 of 8", and **Format Page Numbers…**, which opens the same **Page Number** window.
