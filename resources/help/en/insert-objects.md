# Icons, barcodes, and other objects
<!-- keywords: icon, symbol, special character, barcode, ISBN, UPC, building block, calendar, border, advertisement, business information, date and time, hyperlink, link, insert file -->

This topic covers the other things you can add from the **Insert** tab. Pictures, shapes, tables, Text Art, and footnotes have their own topics.

## Icons

1. Click **Insert > Illustrations > Icons**.
2. In the **Insert Icons** window, type a word such as arrow, mail, or star.
3. Click an icon. Hold **Ctrl** or **Shift** while you click to choose several.
4. Click **Insert**.

JeffPub has more than 1,500 icons from the Lucide set. Each one lands in the middle of the page, one inch tall, in your color scheme's main color. An icon is a shape, so you can recolor it with **Shape Outline** on the **Shape Format** tab.

## Barcodes

1. Click **Insert > Illustrations > Barcode**.
2. In the **Insert Barcode** window, choose a **Type**: Book (ISBN), EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8, Code 128, or Code 39.
3. Type the number or text. For a book, type the ISBN. JeffPub puts in the hyphens.
4. For a book, choose a price add-on or **No add-on**. For EAN and UPC codes, you can type a 2-digit or 5-digit add-on.
5. Check the preview, then click **Insert**.

If something is wrong, the window says so in red and **Insert** stays dimmed. You can also set **Magnification** and **Bar height**. Keep **White background** on, because scanners need the clear space. To change a barcode later, double-click it. To place hyphens correctly for every country, JeffPub downloads the International ISBN Agency's range list and keeps a copy.

## Building blocks

A building block is a ready-made set of objects, such as a heading or a coupon. Open a gallery in **Insert > Building Blocks**:

- **Page Parts**: headings, pull quotes, sidebars, two- and three-column stories, and a contents list. A pull quote is a short quote from the story, set large.
- **Calendars**: this month or next month. For another month, click **More Calendars…**, then pick a month, year, and design in the **Calendar** window.
- **Borders & Accents**: bars, rules, frames, and corner pieces.
- **Advertisements**: a coupon, sale ad, event ad, starburst, price tag, and more.

Click a block to add it. It uses your color scheme and fonts. It is made of ordinary text boxes and shapes, so change it like anything else.

## Business information

Click **Insert > Text > Business Information** and choose a detail, such as Organization Name, Address, Phone, Website, or Logo. If the cursor is in a text box, the detail goes there. If not, JeffPub adds a new text box. Choose **Edit Business Information…** to fill in or change your details. Details you inserted update when you change them.

## Text, links, and other items

- **Insert > Text > Insert File** brings in text from a .txt, .rtf, .html, .docx, or .md file.
- **Symbol** inserts a special character, such as © or é. Click in a text box first.
- **Date & Time**: click in a text box first, choose a format, and check **Update automatically** to keep it current.
- **Insert > Links > Link** (Ctrl+K) makes a hyperlink to a web page, a page in your publication, or an email address. Select text or an object first. Choose **Remove Link** to take it off.
- **Bookmark** gives a name to the selected text or object.
- **Object** lets you choose a file and places it as a picture.
