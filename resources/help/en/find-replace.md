# Find and replace text
<!-- keywords: find, search, replace, find and replace, search text, replace all, change word, match case, whole word, find next, locate text -->

The **Find and Replace** pane looks for words in your whole publication and can swap them for other words. It opens on the right side of the window.

## Find text

1. Press Ctrl+F, or click **Home > Editing > Find**.
2. Type the words you want in **Find what**.
3. Click **Find Next**, or press Enter.

JeffPub jumps to the next match, changes pages if it has to, and selects the words. Click **Find Next** again for the one after that. When it reaches the end, it starts over from the beginning. If there is no match at all, the pane tells you.

JeffPub searches every text box, shape, and table, on every page and every master page. A master page is a background page whose items show on the pages that use it.

## Replace text

1. Press Ctrl+H, or click **Home > Editing > Replace**.
2. Type the words to look for in **Find what** and the new words in **Replace with**.
3. Click **Find Next** to see the first match.
4. Click **Replace** to swap it and move on to the next match. Or click **Find Next** to leave it alone and move on.

To change every match at once, click **Replace All**. The pane tells you how many replacements it made. If it was a mistake, click **Undo** at the top of the window. One undo takes back the whole **Replace All**.

If you click **Replace** before any match is selected, JeffPub only finds the first match. Click **Replace** again to swap it.

## Narrow the search

Check these boxes under **Replace with**:

- **Match case** finds "Fall" but not "fall".
- **Find whole words only** finds "art" but not "party".
- **Search up** looks backward from where you are, toward the start.

**Replace All** ignores **Search up** and always covers the whole publication.
