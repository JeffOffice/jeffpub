# View tab
<!-- keywords: view, view tab, zoom, rulers, show, hide, pages list, scratch area, boundaries, guides, baselines, master page view, two-page spread, new window, measurement -->

The **View** tab is always on the ribbon. Use it to change how the page looks on screen, and to zoom, switch views, and open more windows. Most of these choices change only what you see on screen.

- **Views** switches between **Normal**, where you work on pages, and **Master Page**, where you edit the background pages. See [Master pages, headers, and page numbers](master-pages).
- **Layout** shows one page at a time with **Single Page**, or two facing pages side by side with **Two-Page Spread**. See [Add, move, and delete pages](pages).
- **Show** turns on or off the things you see while you work: **Boundaries** (dotted lines around objects), **Guides**, **Fields**, **Rulers**, **Page Navigation** (the Pages list), **Scratch Area**, **Baselines**, **Graphics Manager**, and **Special Characters**. See [Rulers, guides, and snapping](guides) and [Find your way around the window](window).
- **Zoom** has a **Zoom** box and the **100%**, **Whole Page**, **Page Width**, and **Selected Objects** buttons. See [Find your way around the window](window).
- **Window** has **New Window**, **Arrange All**, **Cascade**, and **Switch Windows** for working with more than one JeffPub window. See [Find your way around the window](window).
- **Tools** has **Measurement**, a small window where you type an object's position, size, and rotation. See [Move, align, group, and layer objects](arrange).
