# Format text
<!-- keywords: font, font size, bold, italic, underline, font color, text color, character spacing, kerning, align text, line spacing, indent, drop cap, format painter, copy formatting, paste formatting, change case -->

You format text on the **Home** tab. The **Font** group changes letters. The **Paragraph** group changes whole paragraphs.

## Choose what to change

- **Letters:** select the text first. If you only click in a word, JeffPub changes that word and the text you type next.
- **Paragraphs:** click in a paragraph, or select several. Alignment, spacing, and indents change every paragraph you touch.
- **Everything in a box:** select the box, not the text.

## Fonts, size, and style

1. In **Home > Font**, pick a font from the font box. The **Scheme Fonts** at the top follow the publication's font scheme. See [Color and font schemes](color-schemes).
2. Type a size in points in the size box, or choose one. A point is 1/72 of an inch.
3. Use **Grow Font** and **Shrink Font** to step the size up or down.

**Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough**, **Subscript**, and **Superscript** are one click each. The arrow beside **Underline** offers dotted, dashed, and wavy lines. **Clear All Formatting** puts text back to what its style says. **Change Case** offers sentence case, lowercase, UPPERCASE, capitalizing each word, toggle case, **Small Caps**, and **All Caps**.

To set many things at once, click the corner button of the **Font** group (or press Ctrl+Shift+F) to open the **Font** dialog.

## Color

Click **Font Color** and pick a scheme, standard, or recent color. **More Colors…** opens the full picker, **Eyedropper** picks up a color from the page, and **Tints…** lists lighter and darker versions of a scheme color. **Automatic** brings back the style's color.

## Character spacing

**Character Spacing** moves letters closer together or farther apart. Choose **Very Tight**, **Tight**, **Normal**, **Loose**, or **Very Loose**. **More Spacing…** opens the **Character Spacing** dialog. There you can also stretch letters, adjust kerning (the gap between two letters), and change word spacing.

## Alignment, spacing, and indents

- **Align Left**, **Center**, **Align Right**, **Justify**, and **Distribute** set how lines line up. **Distribute** is like **Justify**, but it also stretches the last line.
- **Line Spacing** sets the gap between lines. **Paragraph Spacing** sets the gap before or after a paragraph.
- **Increase Indent** and **Decrease Indent** move a paragraph by a quarter inch.
- For exact numbers, choose **Paragraph…** in either spacing menu, or click the corner button of the **Paragraph** group.

## Effects, drop caps, and typography

On the **Text Box** tab, **Effects** adds shadow, outline, emboss, engrave, glow, and gradient fills to letters. A drop cap is a large first letter: click in the paragraph and choose **Text Box > Typography > Drop Cap**. Pick 2 to 5 lines, or **Custom Drop Cap…**. The rest of **Typography**, such as ligatures and stylistic sets, depends on what the font offers.

## Copy formatting with Format Painter

1. Click in text that looks the way you want.
2. Click **Home > Clipboard > Format Painter**.
3. Click the text box you want to change.

The whole box takes on the look of the first text, plus its fill and outline. Hold Shift as you click the button to keep painting. Press Esc to stop.

From the keyboard: select the text or object whose look you want and press **Ctrl+Shift+C** to copy its formatting. Then select other text, or other objects, and press **Ctrl+Shift+V** to give them that look.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- Bold, Italic, Underline: Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U
- Align Left, Center, Align Right, Justify: Ctrl+L, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+R, Ctrl+J
- Grow Font, Shrink Font: Ctrl+Shift+>, Ctrl+Shift+<
- Clear All Formatting: Ctrl+Space
