# Find your way around the window
<!-- keywords: ribbon, tabs, toolbar, interface, screen layout, pages list, thumbnails, status bar, zoom, rulers, task pane, quick access toolbar, collapse ribbon, minimize ribbon, parts of the window -->

The JeffPub window has five main parts: the ribbon across the top, the Pages list on the left, the page area in the middle, task panes on the right, and the status bar along the bottom. The title bar shows the name of your publication, with a star (*) when it has changes you have not saved.

## The ribbon

The ribbon is the strip of buttons at the top. Its buttons are sorted into **tabs**, such as **Home**, **Insert**, and **View**. Click a tab to see its buttons. Each tab splits its buttons into **groups**, such as **Font** and **Paragraph**. Some groups have a small button with three dots in the corner. Click it to open a window with more choices for that group.

More tabs appear when you select something. Select a picture, and **Picture Format** shows up. The others are **Text Box**, **Shape Format**, **Table Design**, **Table Layout**, and **Text Art Format**. **Master Page** appears while you edit master pages. These tabs have a colored dot.

To make more room for the page, collapse the ribbon. Click the arrow at the far right of the tab row, double-click a tab, or press Ctrl+F1. Only the tab names stay. Click a tab to show the buttons again. The **?** button next to the arrow opens Help.

## The File button and the Quick Access Toolbar

The **File** button is left of the tabs. It opens the File page, where you start, open, save, print, and share. Press Esc or click **Back to publication** to return. See [File tab](tab-file).

The Quick Access Toolbar is the row of four small buttons left of the **File** button. It holds **Save**, **Undo**, **Redo**, and **Print**.

## The Pages list

The Pages list shows a small picture and number for each page. Click a page to go to it. Drag a picture up or down to move a page. Right-click for a menu to add, copy, delete, rename, or move pages. To hide or show this list, click **View > Show > Page Navigation**. See [pages](pages).

## The page area

The page area shows the current page like a sheet of paper. The space around it is the scratch area, where you can park objects while you decide where they go. Use the mouse wheel to scroll. Hold Shift and turn the wheel to scroll sideways. Hold Ctrl and turn it to zoom.

Rulers run along the top and the left side. To hide or show them, click **View > Show > Rulers**. Drag from a ruler onto the page to make a guide. See [rulers, guides, and snapping](guides).

## Task panes

Some tools open in a pane on the right: [Find and Replace](find-replace), [Design Checker](design-checker), [Mail Merge](mail-merge), Research, Graphics Manager, Online Pictures, Catalog Merge, and Help. Click the **X** at the top of a pane to close it.

## The status bar

The status bar runs along the bottom. At the left are **Previous Page** and **Next Page** buttons with the page number, such as "Page: 2 of 6". Next to them are the position of the mouse pointer and the size of the selected object. At the right are the **Single Page** and **Two-Page Spread** buttons, the zoom controls, and the **Whole Page** button. The zoom controls are **Zoom Out**, a slider, **Zoom In**, and the zoom percent.
