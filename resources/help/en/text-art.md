# Add Text Art
<!-- keywords: wordart, word art, fancy text, curved text, arch text, logo text, text effects, stylized text, banner text, shaped text, headline, text art styles -->

Text Art is fancy text for short phrases, such as a title, a logo, or a banner. You can fill the letters with color, outline them, bend them into an arch or a wave, and add a shadow. Text Art is drawn as a shape, so it does not flow from box to box like regular text.

## Insert Text Art

1. Click **Insert > Text > Text Art**. A gallery of 30 styles opens.
2. Click a style. JeffPub places Text Art on the page and opens the **Edit Text Art Text** dialog.
3. Type your words. Press Enter for a new line.
4. Choose a **Font** and **Size**, and check **Bold** or **Italic** if you like.
5. Click **OK**.

The style sets the font, the colors, and sometimes a shape. You can change any of them later.

## Edit the words

Select the Text Art and press Enter or F2, or double-click it. You can also click **Text Art Format > Text Art Text > Edit Text**. If you change the size here, the frame grows or shrinks to match.

## Change the look

Click the Text Art. The **Text Art Format** tab appears. See [The Text Art tab](tab-text-art).

- **Style:** pick another one from the **Text Art Styles** gallery. A new style replaces the font, colors, and shape you chose before.
- **Color:** use **Shape Fill** for the inside of the letters and **Shape Outline** for the edge.
- **Shape:** click **Change Shape** and choose Arch Up, Circle, Wave 1, Inflate, Slant Up, Chevron Up, Can Up, Fade Right, and others. **Plain Text** takes the shape away.
- **Spacing:** click **Spacing** and choose **Very Tight**, **Tight**, **Normal**, **Loose**, or **Very Loose**.
- **Even Height:** makes every letter the same height, so small letters grow to match capitals.
- **Text Art Vertical Text:** stacks the letters from top to bottom.
- **Align Text:** with more than one line, picks **Left Align**, **Center**, or **Right Align**. **Letter Justify** and **Stretch Justify** stretch short lines to the width of the longest.

## Size and move

Drag a corner handle to stretch the Text Art. The letters stretch with the frame. Hold Shift while you drag to keep its proportions. See [Select, move, resize, rotate, align, group, and layer](arrange).

## Add a shadow or a 3-D look

On the **Text Art Format** tab, in the **Effects** group:

- **Shadow Effects** has **Outer Shadow**, **Hard Shadow**, **Soft Glow Shadow**, **Shadow Down-Left**, and **Shadow Options…**.
- **3-D Effects** has rotations such as **Perspective Left** and **Off Axis**, and bevels such as **Circle** and **Convex**.
