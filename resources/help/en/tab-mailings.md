# Mailings tab
<!-- keywords: mailings tab, mail merge tab, recipients, merge fields, labels, form letters, ribbon -->

The **Mailings** tab is always on the ribbon. It has the tools for mail merge, which fills one publication with details from a list of people. To open it from the keyboard, press **Alt**, then **M**.

- **Start**: start the Mail Merge wizard or a merge to email, pick or type a recipient list, and edit it. See [Mail merge and catalog merge](mail-merge).
- **Write & Insert Fields**: add an address block, a greeting line, a merge field, or a picture field. See [Mail merge and catalog merge](mail-merge).
- **Preview Results**: see real details in place of the fields, and flip through the records. See [Mail merge and catalog merge](mail-merge).
- **Finish**: merge to the printer, a new publication, a PDF, or email, or export the list. See [Mail merge and catalog merge](mail-merge).
