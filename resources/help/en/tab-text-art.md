# Text Art Format tab
<!-- keywords: text art format, text art tools, wordart tab, contextual tab, text art styles, change shape, shadow effects, 3-D effects -->

The **Text Art Format** tab appears, under the label **Text Art Tools**, when you select Text Art. It goes away when you select something else.

- **Text Art Text** has **Edit Text**, **Spacing**, **Even Height**, **Text Art Vertical Text**, and **Align Text**. See [Add Text Art](text-art).
- **Text Art Styles** has the gallery of 30 styles, **Shape Fill**, **Shape Outline**, and **Change Shape**, which bends the letters into an arch, wave, circle, and more. See [Add Text Art](text-art).
- **Effects** has **Shadow Effects** and **3-D Effects**. See [Add Text Art](text-art).
- **Arrange** controls text wrap, stacking order, grouping, alignment, and rotation. See [Move, align, group, and layer objects](arrange).
- **Size** sets the height and width. See [Move, align, group, and layer objects](arrange).
