# Color and font schemes
<!-- keywords: color scheme, theme, palette, font scheme, fonts, background, page background, gradient, texture, pattern, change template, accent colors, heading font, body font, restyle -->

A color scheme is a set of colors that your whole publication shares. A font scheme is a pair of fonts, one for headings and one for body text. Switching a scheme restyles the publication in one step. The tools are on the **Page Design** tab.

## Choose a color scheme

Click a scheme in the gallery in **Page Design > Schemes**. There are more than 90.

Each scheme has eight colors: Main, Accent 1 through Accent 5, Hyperlink, and Followed Hyperlink. When you color an object with one of these, the object follows the scheme. For example, a banner filled with Accent 1 changes color when you pick a new scheme. A color you mixed yourself stays the same.

## Make your own color scheme

1. Click **Create New Color Scheme…** at the bottom of the gallery.
2. In the **Create New Color Scheme** window, click each color button and pick a color.
3. Type a **Color scheme name** and click **OK**.

The new colors apply to your publication right away.

## Choose a font scheme

Click **Page Design > Schemes > Fonts**. Each choice shows the scheme name and its two fonts, such as "Modern — Montserrat / Open Sans". Text that uses the scheme fonts changes. Text set to a specific font does not.

- **Create New Font Scheme…**: pick a **Heading font**, a **Body font**, and a name.
- **Update Font Scheme**: removes fixed fonts from your styles, so text in those styles uses the scheme fonts.

## Set a page background

1. Go to the page.
2. Click **Page Design > Page Background > Background**.
3. Choose **No Background**, a solid color, or a gradient (a color that fades into another). The colors come from your color scheme.

At the bottom of the gallery, **More Backgrounds…** opens the **Format Background** window, with tabs for Gradient, Texture, Picture, Pattern, and Tint. **Apply Background to All Pages** copies this background to every page.

To use a picture, select it and click **Page Design > Page Background > Apply Image as Background**. The picture becomes the background, and the picture object is removed.

A background belongs to a page. A background on a master page shows on every page that uses that master, unless the page has its own. A master page is a background page whose contents repeat on other pages.

## Change the template

**Page Design > Template > Change Template** opens the **New** page of the File view, with the gallery of designs. Picking a design starts a new publication. It does not restyle the one you have open. To restyle an open publication, change its schemes and backgrounds.
