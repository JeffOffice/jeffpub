# Shape Format tab
<!-- keywords: shape format tab, drawing tools, shape fill, shape outline, shape effects, lines, arrows, ribbon, contextual tab -->

The **Shape Format** tab appears when you select a shape, a line, a group, or several objects at once. Use it to draw, color, and arrange them.

- **Insert Shapes**: draw a shape, change a shape into another one, edit its points, or draw a text box. See [Draw shapes and lines](shapes).
- **Shape Styles**: choose a style, a fill, an outline, and effects such as shadow and glow. See [Draw shapes and lines](shapes).
- **Lines**: draw a line, an arrow, or a double arrow. See [Draw shapes and lines](shapes).
- **Arrange**: wrap text, change the stacking order, group, align, and rotate. See [Move, align, group, and layer objects](arrange) and [Wrap text around objects](wrap).
- **Size**: type an exact height and width. See [Move, align, group, and layer objects](arrange).
