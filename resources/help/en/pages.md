# Add, move, and delete pages
<!-- keywords: add page, insert page, new page, delete page, remove page, duplicate page, copy page, reorder pages, move page, rename page, page list, thumbnails, spread, facing pages, go to page -->

Most publications have more than one page. This topic shows how to add pages, put them in order, and name or delete them.

## Move around your pages

The page list on the left shows a small picture of each page. Click one to go to it. If you do not see the list, turn on **View > Show > Page Navigation**.

You can also use the arrows in the status bar, press **Ctrl+Page Down** or **Ctrl+Page Up**, or press **F5** to type a page number.

## Add pages

- **Insert > Pages > Insert Blank Page** (Ctrl+Shift+N) adds a blank page after the current one.
- The arrow beside that button offers **Insert Duplicate Page** (Ctrl+Shift+U), which adds a copy of the current page, and **Insert Page…**.

In the **Insert Page** window you can set:

- **Number of new pages**.
- **Before current page** or **After current page**.
- What goes on them: blank pages, one text box on each page, or a copy of every object from a page you pick.
- The **Master page** they use. A master page is a background page that repeats its contents on every page that uses it.

The two quick buttons give the new page the same master as the page you are on.

## Reorder pages

- Drag a page in the page list to a new spot.
- Or click **Page Design > Pages > Move Page…**, type the new position, and click **OK**.

## Name a page

Click **Page Design > Pages > Rename Page…**, type a title, and click **OK**. The title shows beside the page number in the page list, such as "3  Menu".

## Delete a page

1. Go to the page.
2. Click **Page Design > Pages > Delete Page**.
3. Click **Yes**.

A publication always keeps at least one page. If you delete a page by mistake, press **Ctrl+Z**.

## Use the right-click menu

Right-click a page in the page list for a menu with **Insert Blank Page**, **Insert Duplicate Page**, **Insert Page…**, **Delete Page**, **Rename Page…**, **Move Page…**, **Master Pages**, and **Two-Page Spread**.

## See two pages side by side

Click **View > Layout > Two-Page Spread** to see pages as an open book. Page 1 stands alone on the right. Then pages 2 and 3 sit together, then 4 and 5, and so on. Click **Single Page** to go back. For a book, pair this with a two-page master.
