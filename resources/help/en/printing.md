# Print a publication
<!-- keywords: print, printer, printing, copies, page range, booklet, poster, banner, tiled, crop marks, bleed, separations, two-sided, print preview, grayscale -->

Printing happens on the **Print** page. You pick your settings on the left and check a preview of each page on the right.

## Print a publication

1. Press Ctrl+P, or click **File > Print**. The **Quick Access Toolbar** has a **Print** button too.
2. Next to the **Print** button, set **Copies**.
3. Choose a **Printer**. The list ends with **Save as PDF**, which makes a PDF file instead of paper.
4. Under **Settings**, choose **Print All Pages**, **Print Current Page**, or **Custom Print**. For a custom print, type one range, such as 2-4, or one page number in the **Pages** box.
5. Choose the other settings you need. They are listed below.
6. Click **Print**.

Click **Printer Properties…** to open the system's print window, with your printer's own options.

The preview on the right has arrows to move from page to page. Check **Show rulers** or **Show page numbers** to see those on the preview.

## Choose a layout

The **Layout** list sets how pages land on paper:

- **One page per sheet** prints each page by itself. A page too big for the paper is printed in pieces. A page a little too big is shrunk to fit.
- **Multiple pages per sheet** puts different pages on one sheet.
- **Multiple copies per sheet** repeats one page across the sheet. Use it for business cards and labels.
- **Booklet, side-fold** and **Booklet, top-fold** put two pages on each sheet in the right order, so a folded stack reads in order. Side-fold puts the two pages side by side. Top-fold puts one above the other.
- **Tiled (posters and banners)** prints a big page in pieces on several sheets, with a small overlap so you can line the sheets up.

## More settings

- **Paper**: Letter, Legal, Tabloid, A4, A3, or **Same as publication**.
- **Sides**: **One-sided**, or two-sided with a flip on the long or the short edge. The preview then tells you which sheet and side each page is on.
- **Color**: **Composite RGB** for full color, or **Composite grayscale** for shades of gray.
- **Print all mail merge records**: available when your publication has a recipient list. See [mail merge](mail-merge).

## Printer's marks and separations

These are for work that goes to a print shop. Ask the shop what it needs.

- **Crop marks** show where to trim. **Bleed marks** show the extra edge. **Registration marks** help line up the colors. **Density bars** and **Color bars** let the shop check the ink. **Job information** prints the name, page, and date.
- **Allow bleeds** prints objects that run off the page, so there is no white edge after trimming. This extra edge is called a **bleed**.
- The marks print outside the page, so use paper larger than your page.
- **Separations (CMYK plates)** under **Color** prints one sheet for each ink: **Cyan**, **Magenta**, **Yellow**, and **Black**, plus any spot colors your publication uses (a spot color is a special premixed ink). Check or clear the plates you want.
