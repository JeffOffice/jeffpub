# Page Design tab
<!-- keywords: page design tab, page layout, page size, margins, orientation, guides, background, color scheme, font scheme, master pages, ribbon -->

The **Page Design** tab is always on the ribbon. Use it to set up the pages of your publication: their size, guides, order, and look.

- **Template**: **Change Template** moves your text and pictures into another design. **Extra Content** opens the pane that holds what had no place in the new design. See [Change a publication's template](change-template).
- **Page Setup**: choose margins, orientation, and page size, and open guide settings. A small button in the corner opens the Page Setup window. See [Page size, margins, and layout](page-setup) and [Rulers, guides, and snapping](guides).
- **Layout**: turn on snapping to guides or to objects. See [Rulers, guides, and snapping](guides).
- **Pages**: delete, move, and rename pages, and choose a master page. See [Add, move, and delete pages](pages) and [Master pages, headers, and page numbers](master-pages).
- **Schemes**: pick a color scheme or a font scheme. See [Color and font schemes](color-schemes).
- **Page Background**: set a color, gradient, or picture behind the page. See [Color and font schemes](color-schemes).
