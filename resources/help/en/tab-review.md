# Review tab
<!-- keywords: review tab, ribbon, proofing, spelling, thesaurus, research, translate, language, word count, design checker, hyphenation -->

The **Review** tab is always on the ribbon. It has the tools that check your words and your design before you share or print.

- **Proofing** has **Spelling**, **Research**, and **Thesaurus**. See [Spelling, thesaurus, research, and hyphenation](proofing).
- **Language** has **Translate**, which opens your selected text in a free translation site, and **Set Proofing Language…**, which tells spelling and hyphenation which language the text is in. See [Spelling, thesaurus, research, and hyphenation](proofing).
- **Check** has **Run Design Checker**, which lists problems such as empty pages. It also has **Word Count**, which shows how many pages, words, characters, paragraphs, text boxes, and pictures you have, and **Hyphenation…**, which sets how words break at line ends. See [Check your design](design-checker).
