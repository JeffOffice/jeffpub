# Use JeffPub with the keyboard
<!-- keywords: keyboard, shortcuts, shortcut keys, hotkeys, key tips, keytips, alt key, f6, tab key, arrow keys, without a mouse, navigate, access keys, focus, ribbon keyboard -->

You can reach the ribbon's commands, the Pages list, and the objects on the page without a mouse. There are three ways: shortcut keys for common commands, KeyTips for the ribbon, and moving around with F6, Tab, and the arrow keys.

## Move between the parts of the window

Press **F6** to move the keyboard **focus** (the place your next key press goes) to the next part of the window. The order is the ribbon, the Pages list, the page, and back to the ribbon. Press **Shift+F6** to go the other way. If the Pages list is hidden, it is skipped. When you use the keyboard, a colored outline shows which control has the focus.

Press **F1** for Help about what you are doing. Press **Ctrl+F1** to collapse or expand the ribbon.

## Use KeyTips on the ribbon

KeyTips are small letters that show up on the ribbon. They let you reach any button without the mouse.

1. Press and release **Alt**. Letters appear on the **File** button, on each tab, and on the Quick Access Toolbar. The Quick Access Toolbar buttons use the numbers 1 to 4.
2. Press the letter of the tab you want, such as **H** for **Home**. The letters for that tab's buttons appear.
3. Type the letters of the button you want. For example, **T** then **B** is **Draw Text Box**.

You can also hold Alt and press a tab's letter in one move. Alt+H opens the **Home** tab. Alt+F opens the **File** page. Alt+Y opens the **Help** tab.

Press **Esc** to go back one step. Press any other key to put the letters away. Letters that look faded belong to a button you can't use now, such as one that needs a selected object.

## Move around the ribbon with the arrow keys

1. Press F6 to land on the current tab.
2. Press **Left** or **Right** to change tabs.
3. Press **Down** to go into the buttons. Use the arrow keys to move between them. **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** also move to the next or the last control.
4. Press **Enter** or **Space** to use a button. A button with a menu opens its menu.
5. Press **Esc** to go back to the page.

## Work on the page

Click the page area, or press F6 until you reach it. Then:

- **Tab** selects the next object. **Shift+Tab** selects the one before it.
- The **arrow keys** move the selected object a small step. Hold **Alt** for the smallest step. To set the step size, see [Change JeffPub's settings](options).
- **Enter** or **F2** edits the text of the selected text box, shape, or table. You can also just start typing.
- **Delete** removes the selected object.
- **Esc** stops editing text. The text box stays selected. Press Esc again to clear the selection.

While you type in a table, **Tab** moves to the next cell. Tab in the last cell adds a row.
