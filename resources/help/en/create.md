# Start a new publication
<!-- keywords: new, new publication, template, templates, blank page, page size, flyer, newsletter, card, brochure, gallery, custom size, my templates, create, design -->

You can start from a **template**, which is a ready-made design with sample text and pictures, or from a blank page. You do this on the **New** page. JeffPub shows this page when it starts.

## Start from a template

1. Click **File**, then **New**. Or press Ctrl+N.
2. Pick a group in the **Categories** list on the left. **Featured** shows a mix. The other groups include Flyers, Newsletters, Brochures, Business Cards, Greeting Cards, Calendars, Invitation Cards, and Resumes. The number next to a group is how many designs it holds.
3. Or type a word in the **Search templates** box.
4. Click a design. A preview appears on the right, with a short description.
5. Click **Create**. You can also double-click the design.

If your current publication has changes you have not saved, JeffPub asks whether to save them first. The new publication then takes its place in the window.

## Choose your look first

The panel on the right lets you change a few things before you click **Create**:

- **Color scheme** and **Font scheme** set the colors and fonts. Leave them at **(template default)** to keep the design's own. See [color and font schemes](color-schemes).
- **Business information** picks the set of contact details the design fills in. Click **Edit…** to change it.
- **Include logo** and **Include mailing address** show only for designs that use them.

You can change the colors and fonts later on the **Page Design** tab, in the **Schemes** group.

## Start from a blank page

1. On the **New** page, click **Featured** or **Blank Sizes**.
2. Click a size. **Featured** starts with a blank letter page, upright or sideways. **Blank Sizes** has paper sizes (such as Letter, Legal, and A4), cards, envelopes, posters, a banner, and sizes for the web.
3. Click **Create**.

## Make your own page size

1. On the **New** page, click **Blank Sizes**.
2. Click **Create New Page Size…** at the end of the list, then click **Create**.
3. Type a name and set the size in the window that opens, then click **OK**.

Your size now appears in the **Blank Sizes** list. Select it and click **Create** to use it. To change the size of a publication you already have, use **Page Design > Page Setup**. See [page setup](page-setup).

## Save your own template

Turn a finished publication into a template you can use again. Click **File > Export > Save as Template**, type a name, and click **OK**. It appears under **My Templates** on the **New** page.
